Privacy Policy

Nivas Hostel Manager. Effective 21 October 2026. Applies to the Nivas app and website for hostel and PG owners in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

Short version: we collect only what the app needs, your hostel records stay on your own device, we do not sell data, we show no ads, and you can delete your account at any time from Profile, Delete account.

1. Who we are and which laws apply

Nivas is published by Ram Laxman Shastry Jagarlapudi, Hyderabad, Telangana, India ("we", "us"). For the account and sign-in data described below we are the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). This policy is written to follow:

  • the DPDP Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (notified 13 November 2025; most notice, consent and rights duties become enforceable on 13 May 2027, and we follow them from now);
  • the Information Technology Act, 2000, including Section 43A and the Reasonable Security Practices and Sensitive Personal Data or Information Rules, 2011, for as long as they remain in force;
  • the CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022 on reporting cyber incidents;
  • other Indian laws that apply to a hostel business in Telangana, explained in section 6.

2. Two different roles

  • Hostel owner (you): you decide which resident details to enter and why. For your residents' details you are the Data Fiduciary, and Nivas is only a tool that helps you keep your own records.
  • Resident: residents do not create accounts. A resident's details appear in Nivas only because the hostel owner entered them. A resident who opens a receipt link sees only that receipt.

3. What we collect and where it is kept

  • Account data (our servers, through Google Firebase Authentication): email address, a user ID, sign-in method (email and password, Google or Apple), whether the email is verified, and sign-in times. Firebase also logs technical data such as IP address for security. Passwords are stored by Firebase in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Business records (your device only): business name, owner name, mobile number, hostel names, addresses, areas, optional map pin, prices and facilities, resident names, mobile numbers, room and sharing details, dues, payments and receipts. These are saved in your browser's storage on your own phone or computer. They are not uploaded to our servers. Clearing browser data, losing the device or deleting your account removes them, so keep your own backup.
  • Payment details you choose to save (your device only): UPI ID, bank account holder name, bank account number and IFSC code. We never ask for card numbers, CVV, PINs, passwords or OTPs. Please do not enter Aadhaar or PAN numbers anywhere in the app; Nivas does not need them.
  • Camera: used only after you tap Start camera or Scan, to read a QR code or barcode. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded.
  • Location: used only when you tap Use current location or Pin exact location. Your coordinates are sent once to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) to find the area name. A pinned location is saved with that hostel on your device.
  • Stored on your device by the app: your chosen area, the app cache (service worker) and sign-in session. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

4. Why we use it, and your consent

We use your data only to: create and secure your account; let you manage hostels, residents, dues and payments; show hostels for the area you pick; create receipts and let you send them from your own WhatsApp or SMS app; and answer support requests. We do not sell or rent personal data, do not profile you and do not show advertisements.

We rely on your consent, given when you tick the box and create an account, and, for the camera and location, when you allow the permission on your device. You may withdraw consent at any time by turning off the permission or by deleting your account. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent. Withdrawal does not affect what was done before it, and some features will stop working.

5. Residents' data and children

  • Enter only what you need to run the hostel, such as name, room, mobile number and payments. Tell each resident what you record and why, and keep their permission.
  • If a resident is under 18, take a parent or guardian's verifiable consent first, as the DPDP Act requires. Do not use resident data to track, profile or advertise to children.
  • Nivas itself is only for people aged 18 or above. If we learn that a child created an account, we delete it.
  • A resident may ask the hostel owner, or write to us, to see, correct or erase their details.

6. Hostel owner duties under Indian and Telangana rules

Nivas is a record-keeping tool. It does not replace the registers, licences and registrations the law requires of you. In particular, for hostels in Hyderabad, Cyberabad and Rachakonda areas you should check and keep the following current:

  • municipal trade licence from the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (or your local municipality);
  • police registration (Form A) for women's hostels and PGs at your local police station, and the resident register and staff background checks the police require;
  • fire safety under the Telangana Fire Services Act, 1999 and the National Building Code;
  • FSSAI registration or licence if you serve food;
  • registration under the Telangana Shops and Establishments Act, 1988 if you employ staff, and GST registration where your turnover requires it.

Rules and local orders change. Confirm the current position with the authority or an advocate or chartered accountant.

7. Receipts and receipt links

When you send a receipt, the receipt details (receipt number, date, hostel name, address and contact numbers, resident name, room, sharing, month, rent, advance, balance, total and your name) are placed inside the link itself. The PDF is built in the resident's browser when they open the link. We do not store receipt PDFs on a server.

Anyone who has the link can read the receipt, and a link cannot be withdrawn after it is sent. Send it only to the resident concerned. The message is sent from your own phone through WhatsApp (Meta) or your SMS app, and their privacy terms and your carrier's charges apply.

8. Who else receives data

  • Google Firebase / Google LLC: sign-in, verification and deletion emails, and hosting if you use Firebase Hosting.
  • Google or Apple: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim): coordinates, only when you ask the app to find your area.
  • Razorpay public IFSC service: only the IFSC code you type, to confirm that the bank branch exists.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs: delivers the PDF, Excel and QR-reading code libraries to your browser; it sees your IP address like any website.
  • Authorities: only where Indian law or a lawful order requires it.

Our service providers may process data on servers outside India. The DPDP Act allows this unless the Central Government restricts a country. We will follow any such restriction.

9. Security and breaches

We use HTTPS, verified email sign-in, strong password rules, Firebase's access controls and the principle of collecting the minimum. Please also lock your phone, keep it updated and do not share your password. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Act and Rules, and report cyber incidents to CERT-In within six hours of noticing them where the Directions apply.

10. How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your Firebase account and remove the Nivas records stored on that device, and we aim to clear any backups within 30 days. We may keep minimal information for as long as a law requires, for example to answer a legal notice or to prove that a deletion was requested. Tax and accounting laws may require you to keep payment records, so keep your own copies before you delete. Receipts you already sent stay with the people who received them.

11. Your rights

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we process and who we share it with;
  • correct, complete or update your data (use Profile, Edit profile for most things);
  • ask for erasure of your data (Profile, Delete account, then confirm with the link we email you);
  • withdraw consent;
  • have a grievance answered quickly;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act.
