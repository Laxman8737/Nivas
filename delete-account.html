Delete your Nivas account

Nivas Hostel Manager. You can delete your account and data at any time. This page is open to everyone, with or without signing in.

Option 1: delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nivas and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile, then Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Send confirmation email.
  4. Open the email we send to your registered address, on the same phone, and tap the link. Your account is deleted when the link opens.

Open the app

Option 2: you cannot open the app

Email lakshman15407@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject Delete my Nivas account. We will confirm by replying to that address and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, user ID, sign-in method).
  • Your business profile, hostels, residents, dues, payments, bills and saved payment details that are stored on the device where you confirm the deletion. Nivas does not keep copies of these on a server, so if you used Nivas on more than one device, clear the app data on the other devices too.

What stays

  • Receipts you already sent: they stay with the people who received them, and links already sent will keep working because the receipt is inside the link.
  • Minimal records that the law requires us to keep, such as proof that you asked for deletion, and backups that are cleared within 30 days.

Before you delete

Deletion cannot be undone. Save your own copy of any bills or payment records you need for tax or accounts.
